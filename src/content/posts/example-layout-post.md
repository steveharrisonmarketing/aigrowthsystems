This post exists so you can see how a blog post looks. It has the status `review`, so it only appears while the site is in review mode and never on the live site.

## How posts work

Each post is one text file in `src/content/posts/`. The top of the file holds the title, description, category, date and status. The rest is the article.

A post goes live when two things are true: its status is `published`, and its publish date has arrived.

## What every post needs

- Something of our own: a build, a number, a screenshot or an opinion.
- A source link for any fact or figure.
- The answer to the reader's question in the first two or three sentences.

## What a reader sees

The title, the date and the reading time sit at the top. The article follows. A box at the end invites them to take the Free AI Growth Audit.
